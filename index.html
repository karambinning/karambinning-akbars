The Shop

The kind of shop a street relies on.

A mini-market is not a brand. It's a habit. Bread on the way to work, milk on the way home, a bottle for Friday, a card for Sunday, a phone-top-up because the kid lost his charger again.

Akbar's sits on Sneinton Dale — a working high street with a long memory — stocking the things a neighbourhood actually buys, day in, day out. Walk in and have a look.

What You'll Find

Stocked for everyday Sneinton.

01 / Aisle

Groceries

Bread, milk, eggs, tinned goods, store-cupboard basics. The everyday list, kept fresh and kept stocked.

02 / Aisle

Off-Licence

Beers, wines and spirits for the weekend table. Challenge 25 — bring ID if you look under 25.

03 / Aisle

Household

Soap powder, dish soap, kitchen roll, batteries, lightbulbs. The things you only remember when you're out.

04 / Aisle

Newsagent

Papers, magazines, cards, sweets and chocolate. The counter staples that have always belonged in a corner shop.

05 / Aisle

World Foods

Rice, lentils, spices, sauces and the ingredients that don't always make the supermarket shelf.

06 / Aisle

Top-Ups & Counter

Phone credit, tobacco, lottery tickets, and the bits and pieces that live behind the counter.
